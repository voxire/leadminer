# 083 — Synthesized S1/S2 Backlog

## Verdict

Across the audit reports reviewed, the highest-risk themes are incorrect success signals and lossy persistence: failed/partial runs can be published, and current merge/write paths can silently corrupt the cumulative master. The website probe also has a serious SSRF/resource-exhaustion surface and cannot reliably distinguish a dead site from a blocked or transiently failing one. This is a deduplicated implementation backlog, not a vote count: repeated independent reports raise confidence, while blast radius captures whether a defect can affect a run, the master, security/legal posture, or a narrower subset.

## Prioritized backlog

Ordering uses **priority = independent reports agreeing × blast radius**. Blast radius is 4 (run/master/security/legal), 3 (large source or lead-quality/cost impact), 2 (bounded operational or export impact), or 1 (localized). Agreement counts distinct report files; the report numbers in parentheses make the count auditable. Fix size: **S** = localized change, **M** = a contained cross-module change, **L** = data-model, policy, or pipeline redesign. Items with the same score are ordered by operational risk.

1. **A binary `website_live` signal promotes blocked/transient sites as dead leads and treats 4xx as live, so the top rebuild pitch is unreliable.** A 403/429, timeout, or TLS/bot block is not proof of a dead site; 404/410 and parked/placeholder HTTP-200 pages also need distinct treatment. **Where:** `enricher.py:145-152, 241-259`; `pitch_recommender.py:52-55`. **Agreement:** 7 reports (005, 020-type-contract, 029, 043, 064, 067-competitive, 073-cost). **Blast / score:** 4 / 28. **Fix size:** M — replace the boolean with explicit probe outcomes (confirmed dead, live, parked, blocked, transient/unknown), classify status/body/transport separately, retry transient failures within budgets, and score only confirmed opportunity.

2. **Dedup chooses every conflicting field from whichever whole record has more populated fields, silently discarding better/fresher values and letting stale master data win.** The same heuristic makes conflict resolution source-agnostic and can depend on record order; sentinel zeros and empty strings further appear populated. **Where:** `dedup.py:41-60`; `main.py:123-126`. **Agreement:** 7 reports (002, 024, 028, 034, 035, 070, 095). **Blast / score:** 4 / 28. **Fix size:** L — use per-field/source/recency survivorship rules, preserve raw observations and conflicts, and distinguish missing/empty/uncomputed values.
