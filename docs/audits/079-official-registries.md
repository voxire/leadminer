# 079 — Official business registries: Lebanon and Saudi Arabia

## Verdict

There is a useful official, free, record-by-record lookup in each market, but neither market offers a verified, documented, free public bulk/API feed of the company records needed to seed leadminer. Lebanon’s Ministry of Justice commercial-register site is the closest public business index; Saudi Arabia’s Ministry of Commerce provides a free CR query, but it is a CAPTCHA-gated web service. CAS, MHRSD, the Saudi Business Center, and Etimad are not substitutes for an open company directory.

“No public API found” below means no public developer documentation or supported unauthenticated endpoint was exposed by the cited official pages during this audit (2026-10-03); it is not a claim that the governments have no private inter-agency APIs.

## Findings

### Lebanon

| Official source | Public API / cost | What is exposed | Programmatic access and fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ministry of Justice — Commercial Register** ([public search](http://cr.justice.gov.lb/); [MoJ department description](https://www.justice.gov.lb/index.php/department-details/4/1)) | Public browser search; no API, documented bulk download, or published machine interface found. The public search page has no sign-in/payment step; it does not publish a query fee schedule. Certified copies/extracts are a separate registry transaction and may be charged. | Search by any part of registration number or trade name. MoJ says the site provides detailed information on merchants, commercial establishments, and companies. Its registry description lists merchant name, trade name, activity/object, headquarters and branch locations, establishment address/name, and authorized signatories. The site does not guarantee every field appears on every company result. | Use the HTML search form at the linked official site for occasional lookups; it is not an API or bulk export. The form is ASP.NET and routes searches to a result page, with no published API specification. Do not build a high-volume scraper around this interface; request data or a permitted extract from the relevant commercial-register secretariat instead. |
| **Central Administration of Statistics (CAS)** ([statistics catalogue](https://www.cas.gov.lb/statistics/); [administrative statistics](https://www.cas.gov.lb/administrative-statistics/); [historical downloads](https://www.cas.gov.lb/statistics/historical-downloads/); [contact/data inquiry](https://www.cas.gov.lb/contact-us/)) | Public statistical tables and reports are downloadable without charge. No public business-record API or establishment-level company index found. | Aggregate official indicators and publications (economic, administrative, labour, population, etc.); public catalogue describes downloadable tables/reports, not named businesses, CR numbers, addresses, or officers. | Download the published report/table files or contact CAS to request available statistics. Appropriate for market sizing and industry/region context, not identifying individual leads. CAS explicitly provides a contact route for data requests. |
| **Ministry of Economy and Trade (MoET) — Companies Department / Trade Information Center** ([Companies Department](https://www.economy.gov.lb/ar/services/دائرة-الشركات/التجارة); [Trade Information Center](https://www.economy.gov.lb/ar/services/مركز-المعلومات-التجارية/التجارة)) | No public general-company API/index found. MoET offers request-based information/certificates and available statistics; pricing depends on the requested formal service and is not stated as a free feed. | The Companies Department page describes foreign-company branches/representative offices, commercial agencies, court notices, and responses to inquiries about foreign company branches/agency status. It is not the national register of all Lebanese businesses. The Trade Information Center is focused on external-trade information/statistics and asks for an information-request form. | Submit an information request through the named department for targeted queries/statistics. Not a practical company discovery source or a programmatic lead feed. |

**Registry coverage caveat:** MoJ’s department description says the register is maintained at each first-instance court, with the relevant secretariat determined by the commercial business location. A search hit should therefore not be treated as a quality-verified, centrally synchronized identity record: MoJ itself notes that registry clerks record submitted information without checking its truth. [MoJ registry description](https://www.justice.gov.lb/index.php/department-details/4/1)

### Saudi Arabia

| Official source | Public API / cost | What is exposed | Programmatic access and fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ministry of Commerce (MoC) — Commercial Registration Data Query** ([English service details](https://mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/ServiceDetails.aspx?sID=91); [service page / Start Service](https://mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/Commercial-data.aspx)) | The MoC labels the query **free**, immediate, and available to consumers and merchants. No public API specification, bulk export, or developer credentials are published on the service page. The online form includes a verification-code/CAPTCHA. | Search is by establishment name or unified national number; MoC says the result is commercial-registration data. The public service description does **not** enumerate a complete response schema, so legal name, CR number/status, activity, address, and officers should each be verified against a sample result rather than assumed. The public service is an individual lookup, not a documented enumeration/search endpoint. | Manual lookups through the Start Service page are supported. A program should not treat the interactive CAPTCHA form as an API or attempt CAPTCHA evasion. For automated access, ask MoC/Saudi Business Center for an authorized integration/data-sharing product; no unauthenticated public endpoint was documented. |
| **Saudi Business Center (SBC)** ([business.sa](https://business.sa/); [MoC service catalogue](https://mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/default.aspx)) | No public company-search API or all-business directory was found. Public-facing portal provides e-services; transactional service access can route through account-authenticated business operations. | SBC facilitates starting and managing a business and CR lifecycle transactions; it is not itself a public index with company records. MoC catalogue links multiple company CR actions to SBC, while the MoC's distinct query above remains the public discovery service. | Use the public service pages for human workflows. For integration, request SBC/MoC partner access and API terms; do not infer a supported public API from the existence of the service portal. |
| **MHRSD / Qiwa (labor and establishment services)** ([MHRSD service catalogue](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services); [Qiwa sign-in](https://sso.qiwa.sa/ar/sign-in)) | No public commercial-registry API or browseable company directory found. MHRSD publishes a public service catalogue, but employer/establishment functions are labor services and use authenticated Qiwa workflows. | MHRSD/Qiwa concern labor establishment files, employer services, work permits, and workforce/labor compliance—not statutory commercial registration. The public service catalogue describes services rather than exposing named business records. It does not provide the MoC's general CR fields as an open dataset. | Use the service catalogue for discovering official labor service routes; Qiwa access is for an authorized employer/user. Obtain CR identity/status from MoC, not by scraping MHRSD or treating a Qiwa establishment record as a full business registry record. |
| **Etimad — government procurement** ([Etimad portal](https://portal.etimad.sa/en-us); [MoC’s official link to Etimad](https://mc.gov.sa/en/Pages/default.aspx)) | Etimad is a procurement/supplier platform, not an open national business register. No public vendor-directory API or free company master-data API was found. Portal entry redirects to Etimad authentication in an unauthenticated session; no API documentation was found. | Supplier account data is supplied in the procurement onboarding/bidding process and is not published as a bulk directory for lead generation. Procurement notices/award information may be public through platform pages, but that is contract/tender data, not a canonical list of registered companies or a profile API. | Companies participate by registering as suppliers and using the Etimad portal to transact/bid. For programmatic procurement records, obtain an authorized integration or use official public notices/exports where offered; do not treat bidder registration as consent to redistribute supplier profiles. |

**Important naming correction:** Saudi Arabia’s commercial register is administered by the Ministry of Commerce, not MHRSD. The MoC service directly answers the CR-data lookup question; MHRSD/Qiwa data can complement it for authorized labor/employer workflows but is not a public commercial registry.

## Not a bug, but worth knowing

- **Field completeness is unevenly documented.** Lebanon’s MoJ describes registration particulars and offers detailed lookup, while Saudi MoC advertises “commercial registration data” but does not publish the response schema on the service page. Validate exactly which fields are returned before mapping either result into `address`, `category`, or contact/person fields.
- **A registry record is not the same as a storefront.** CR data verifies legal/commercial existence and may give registered activity/address, but does not establish that a business is operating, has a public-facing location, has a reachable phone, or lacks a website.
- **Third-party directories are not official registry APIs.** Do not present private business indexes or aggregators as official government coverage; their reuse rights, freshness, and field provenance need separate verification.

## Recommended order of work

1. **Saudi:** add MoC's free manual lookup as a verification/backfill workflow keyed by unified national number/name; first capture a few authorized sample results and document the actual exposed fields. Treat CAPTCHA and lack of API docs as a hard stop for unattended scraping.
2. **Lebanon:** use the MoJ portal for targeted verification by name/registration number. For bulk access, request a lawful data extract or data-sharing arrangement from the commercial-register secretariat; do not assume a national API exists.
3. **Use CAS only for aggregate enrichment** (industry, region, or market context), and MoET/Etimad/MHRSD only for their respective scoped records—not as replacements for company discovery.
